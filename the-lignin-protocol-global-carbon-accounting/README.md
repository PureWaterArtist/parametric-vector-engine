### 🧮 Core Engineering Equations

#### 1. Total Architectural Column Load ($M_{\text{total}}$)
Calculates the absolute physical compression weight resting on a node's foundational framework during an active production cycle:
$$M_{\text{total}} = M_{\text{baseline}} + \sum (E_{\text{untreated}} \cdot \beta_{\text{carbon}}) - L_{\text{sequestration}}$$
*   $M_{\text{baseline}}$: The static structural weight of the unloaded hardware/infrastructure.
*   $E_{\text{untreated}}$: Metric tons of active raw greenhouse gas gases emitted.
*   $\beta_{\text{carbon}}$: Gravimetric scaling coefficient (translates gas volume into simulated mechanical tons).
*   $L_{\text{sequestration}}$: Certified physical mass removed via long-term, structural wood-fiber synthesis.

#### 2. Structural Column Compression Strain ($\epsilon$)
Measures the physical material deformation ratio under the current gravimetric carbon load:
$$\epsilon = \left( \frac{M_{\text{total}}}{M_{\text{critical}}} \right)^2$$
*   $M_{\text{critical}}$: The ultimate material yield limit where physical structural fracturing and collapse occur.

#### 3. Operational Efficiency Throttle Factor ($\Phi$)
Dictates the real-time system clock speed degradation dynamically based on mechanical material strain:
$$\Phi = 1.0 - \epsilon$$

*   **Stable Infrastructure ($\epsilon \to 0$):** $\Phi \to 1.0$, allowing full hardware lines to operate at peak nominal execution speed ($4.0\text{ GHz}$).
*   **Systemic Structural Fault ($\epsilon \ge 1.0$):** $\Phi \to 0$, forcing an immediate **Autonomous Lockout Threshold** that freezes processing lines.
