### 🏛️ The Physics of Capillary Tension

Instead of a central authority shutting down trade routes during a crisis, this engine replicates the **capillary tension** of drawing water from drying forest soil. 

When a node's local water reserves drop toward its ecological survival floor, the physical tension of the soil matrix climbs exponentially. The system must exert massive amounts of mechanical or technological energy to extract a single drop of liquid. When this energy cost exceeds kinetic limits, the trade route **automatically freezes on its own**, safely shielding the local human population and ecosystem.

### 🧮 Core System Equations

#### 1. Capillary Soil Tension Factor ($T_i$)
Calculates the automated thermodynamic friction encountered during water extraction at Node $i$:
$$T_i = \left( \frac{W_{\text{optimal}}}{W_{\text{current}} - W_{\text{floor}}} \right)^2$$

*   **Abundant Water:** As $W_{\text{current}} \to W_{\text{optimal}}$, Tension $T_i \to 1.0$ (Frictionless, high-efficiency extraction).
*   **Extreme Scarcity:** As $W_{\text{current}} \to W_{\text{floor}}$, Tension $T_i \to \infty$ (Exponentially mounting energy barriers).

#### 2. Actual Transaction Energy Cost ($E_{\text{total}}$)
The final physical energy expenditure required to clear a virtual water export package across an edge pathway:
$$E_{\text{total}} = V_b \cdot P_{\text{crop}} \cdot T_i$$

*   $V_b$: Total volumetric Blue Water requirement of the commodity weight.
*   $P_{\text{crop}}$: The node's current localized genetic crop portfolio allocation score.
