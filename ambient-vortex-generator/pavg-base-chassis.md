- THE 42.5-DEGREE COANDA WALL RUNWAYS:
  * The interior walls of the compression chamber feature microscopic, stepped 
    surface ridges modeled after transcendental Pi (π). 
  * As the air spins inward, these ridges create miniature rolling boundary layers 
    (the Coanda Effect), forcing the air column to slide over itself with near-
    zero surface drag or wall friction.

- THE PRIME-NUMBER RESONANCE DAMPENERS:
  * Six independent sockets are bored into the bottom foundation layer, positioned 
    strictly along prime angular offsets (0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180°, 225°).
  * These sockets are injected with flexible, conductive carbon black TPU. 
  * They act as active acoustic traps, catching high-velocity harmonic vibrations 
    generated by the internal shear walls and grounding them safely into the 
    mounting surface before they can cause physical chassis warp.

- THE ACCELERATION APERTURE:
  * At the absolute center, the base plate tapers into an airtight, polished 
    funnel measuring exactly 38.2mm wide (100 / φ²). 
  * This geometric restriction forces the inward-moving air to compress, creating 
    the rapid pressure drop required to initiate the self-sustaining siphon loop.
